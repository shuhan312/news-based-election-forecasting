## Methodology

Each row is a material spatial-overlap candidate, not a final mapping. A candidate is classified as exact or near_exact only when it is one-to-one and meets the stated mutual-coverage threshold. One-to-many relationships are classified as split; many-to-one relationships as merged; many-to-many or weaker one-to-one relationships as uncertain.

## Acceptance criteria and limitations

No row is accepted by this report. A later decision requires both the retained GIS evidence and direct official legal boundary evidence for the individual pair. A shared name, the largest overlap, or spatial area alone is insufficient. The report also does not establish comparable electorates or voting geography.

All decisions remain `requires_review`. GIS and legal boundary evidence are retained in every row, but no historical comparison is permitted until a future explicit approval process records a final mapping decision.
