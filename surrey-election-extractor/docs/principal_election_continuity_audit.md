# Surrey Principal Election Legal-Continuity Audit

## Decision

The 2013→2017 and 2017→2021 principal-election references are approved only because the configured statutory evidence identifies the 2012 arrangements as the divisions replaced by the 2024 Order at the May 2025 elections, and every transition has exactly 81 matching published division names.

This is not a 2021→2026 boundary equivalence claim. The existing 2026 GIS permission audit remains separate and unchanged.

## Coverage

- Approved transitions: 2
- Approved division-level prior references: 162
- Exact-label party-history references: 706
- Candidate-identity references created: 0
- Incumbency references created: 0
- Party vote-share change values created: 0

## Boundaries

Only source-reported Elected rows can supply a previous winner. Exact original party labels can support party-history fields. Candidate identity, incumbency, party totals, vote-share change, swing, redistribution and vote-ranking margins remain NULL/unavailable.

## Sources

- [surrey-electoral-changes-order-2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2012/1872/contents/made): The Surrey (Electoral Changes) Order 2012 provided the existing County Council divisions that remained in use before the later 2024 review.
- [surrey-electoral-changes-order-2024-explanatory-memorandum](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2024/1177/pdfs/uksiem_20241177_en_001.pdf): Paragraphs 4.1 and 5.1 state that the new arrangements were scheduled for May 2025 and that the existing divisions provided under the 2012 Order would be replaced by 81 new divisions.
- [lgbce-surrey-final-recommendations-2024](https://www.lgbce.org.uk/sites/default/files/2024-05/surrey_fr_long_report_-_final.pdf): Paragraphs 7 to 11 state that Surrey's last review was completed in 2012 and that the 2024 review proposed changes to most divisions.
