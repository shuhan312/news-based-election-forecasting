# Historic Surrey (2013–2021) to 2026 Historical Reference Permission Audit

## Purpose

This audit separately records whether an existing `accepted_direct` GIS relationship may be used as a limited analytical historical reference. It does not change election results, assert legal succession, or transfer candidate identity, incumbency, swing, or redistributed votes.

## Result

- Accepted-direct relationships reviewed: 22
- Approved for limited historical reference: 22
- Insufficient official evidence: 0
- Other retained crosswalk relationships, not reclassified: 145

Approved records may expose only the previous principal event, its source-reported winning candidate and party (without asserting identity continuity), source-reported turnout and electorate, source-reported winner vote share, observed contest size, and exact original-party history. Every other crosswalk status remains blocked. Candidate identity, incumbency, predecessor relationships, party vote-share change, swing, and vote redistribution remain unavailable for every record.

## Official evidence registry

- [surrey-structural-changes-order-2026](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukdsi/2026/9780348278507/pdfs/ukdsi_9780348278507_en.pdf): Articles 47(2)(c) and 49(2)(c) state that each 2026 East or West Surrey ward has the same area as the county council electoral division of that name under the 2024 Order.
- [surrey-electoral-changes-order-2024](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukdsi/2024/9780348262599/pdfs/ukdsi_9780348262599_en.pdf): Article 3 and Schedule 1 establish the 81 2024 Surrey county electoral divisions and the Order map defines each division's area.
- [lgbce-surrey-final-recommendations-2024](https://www.lgbce.org.uk/sites/default/files/2024-05/surrey_fr_long_report_-_final.pdf): Paragraph 11 states that 24 Surrey division boundaries would stay the same; the report directs readers to the large PDF or digital mapping for the intended boundary detail.
- [ons-surrey-county-electoral-divisions-may-2023](https://services1.arcgis.com/ESMARspQHYMw9BZ9/arcgis/rest/services/County_Electoral_Division_May_2023_Boundaries_EN_BFC/FeatureServer/0/query?where=CED23CD%20BETWEEN%20%27E58001460%27%20AND%20%27E58001541%27&outFields=CED23NM%2CCED23CD&returnGeometry=true&outSR=27700&f=geojson): The official ONS May 2023 County Electoral Division geometry supplies the historical Surrey division code, name and boundary used by the 2013, 2017 and 2021 result records before the 2024 review took effect.
- [surrey-east-wards-gis-2026](https://sccmaps.surreycc.gov.uk/webmaps/rest/services/Elections_map_2026/East_Surrey_Council_ward/MapServer/0/query?where=1%3D1&outFields=Name&returnGeometry=true&outSR=27700&f=geojson): The Surrey County Council East Surrey Council ward layer publishes the 2026 ward geometry and name used in the election.
- [surrey-west-wards-gis-2026](https://sccmaps.surreycc.gov.uk/webmaps/rest/services/Elections_map_2026/West_Surrey_Council_wards/MapServer/0/query?where=1%3D1&outFields=Name&returnGeometry=true&outSR=27700&f=geojson): The Surrey County Council West Surrey Council wards layer publishes the 2026 ward geometry and name used in the election.

## Boundary of this decision

The 2026 Structural Changes Order links each 2026 ward to a same-named 2024 division. The 2024 Electoral Changes Order replaced the former divisions, so this audit does not treat a matching name as proof. Approval is granted only where the existing crosswalk contains one exact accepted-direct GIS relationship and the configured official legal and mapping evidence is retained. This is analytical geographic continuity, not a legal or personal identity claim.
